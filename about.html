About FarmLife

FarmLife is a unified livestock intelligence platform built to secure your herd, streamline farm operations, and connect your community with timely insights and support.

Explore Home Open Dashboard

What FarmLife delivers

Complete Farm Control

From theft reporting and community alerts to livestock inventory, analytics, and secure notifications, FarmLife brings all farm management capabilities into one modern, easy-to-use system.

  • Real-time theft reporting: Submit incidents, monitor case status, and connect with recovery teams instantly.
  • Community intelligence: Share alerts, local reports, and herd updates across trusted networks.
  • Inventory & sales management: Track herd value, readiness, and upcoming sales in one place.
  • Insights & analytics: See health trends, risk signals, and operational performance with clear dashboards.
  • Secure alerts: Stay notified of critical events, system updates, and recovery actions without missing a beat.
