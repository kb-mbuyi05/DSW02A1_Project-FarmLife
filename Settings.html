Resetting your password

Quick recovery

Forgot your password? FarmLife makes account recovery simple and secure so you can get back to managing your herd without delay.

  1. Open the Log in page and click Forgot Password.
  2. Enter the email address or phone number linked to your FarmLife account.
  3. Check your inbox or SMS for a one-time recovery link.
  4. Follow the reset instructions to choose a new secure password.
  5. Sign in again and update your account settings if needed.

Tip: choose a strong password with at least 8 characters, numbers, and a symbol to keep your farm data protected.

How to use FarmLife

User guide

FarmLife is built to make livestock monitoring, theft reporting, inventory control, and farm communication feel effortless.

Start from the Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a quick overview of alerts, reports, herd status, and your next actions.

Submit theft reports

Use the Report page to log stolen livestock details and share them with your local farm community.

Monitor inventory

Track animals, sales, and inventory readiness in the Inventory & Sales section for smarter herd management.

Review community alerts

Stay connected through the Community page and receive safety warnings from other farmers.

Settings tips & best practices

Farm-ready

These quick settings actions help you stay secure and make better use of FarmLife every day.

  • Update your farm contact details and emergency channels regularly.
  • Choose the notification types that matter most: theft, health, or system alerts.
  • Share access with only trusted staff and review permissions often.
  • Bookmark the Web Dashboard and download the Mobile App for on-the-go updates.
