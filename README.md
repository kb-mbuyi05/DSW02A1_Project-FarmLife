# DSW02A1_Project-FarmLife
Problem Statement: Livestock theft is a major issue in rural communities, especially in South Africa. Farmers lose significant income. The theft of livestock is often organized by syndicates, making recovery difficult. Response times are slow, and farmers lack real-time monitoring tools. Traditional record-keeping is inefficient and prone to errors. Farmers often work in isolation, without coordinated community defence or shared intelligence. Solution Statement: We, Cold_Blooded have brought the solution to this, Project FarmLife. A digital, technology-driven platform that empowers farmers to protect, monitor, and manage their herds/livestock. It combines GPS tracking, community reporting, and data analytics into one system. Functionality: Security: Real-time GPS tracking of animals, boundary alerts, and theft incident reporting. Community Collaboration: Farmers can share theft reports, sightings, and coordinate responses. Health Management: Automated vaccination reminders, disease outbreak alerts, and medical history tracking. Business Insights: Inventory management, sales tracking, and market value forecasting. Offline Access: Farmers can still use the system in areas with poor connectivity, synching data later.

Core Functionality:

Sign Up and Login Farmers create accounts securely. Login system ensures only verified users access sensitive data.

Homepage Introduces the platform’s mission. Shows statistics (registered farmers, theft reduction). Provides quick-action buttons: Register Herd, Report Theft, View Dashboard.

Animal Profiles Detailed records for each animal (breed, age, weight, vaccination schedule). Notes on behaviour and grazing preferences. Easy updates and management.

GPS Tracking Dashboard Live map showing animal locations. Movement history logs (pasture entry, water station visits, boundary alerts). Battery status of GPS tags.

Alerts and Notifications Security alerts (unusual movement, theft attempts). Health alerts (vaccination due, disease outbreak). Breeding cycle notifications. System alerts (low battery, offline sync complete).

Community Theft Reports Farmers report theft incidents with details (location, description, vehicle sightings). Verified/unverified status for reports. Map visualisation of theft hotspots. Community collaboration through comments and confirmations.

Inventory and Sales Track herd population and market value. Record livestock sales with buyer details and transaction logs. Forecast market trends (e.g: beef price increases). Export sales data for record-keeping.

Insights and Analytics Health index and security status of the herd. Breeding and growth forecasts. Theft hotspot heatmaps. Regional security trends.

Settings Manage account profile (name, email, phone). Configure alert preferences. Set up farm zones and grazing boundaries. Security and password management.

Offline Ready Progressive Web App (PWA) technology for offline use. Local caching of animal records and maps. Background sync when internet is available. Storage management tips for efficient offline operation.
