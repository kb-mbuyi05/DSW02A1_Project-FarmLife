Platform services

Connected tools

FarmLife provides a powerful digital ecosystem that supports your farm across every device and every workflow. These services make your operations smoother, safer, and more connected.

  • Web Dashboard: Use the main dashboard for real-time monitoring, alerts, reports, and action planning from any browser.
  • Mobile App: Stay mobile with herd alerts, theft reports, and farm notifications while you are in the field.
  • Farmer Portal: Securely share access with trusted staff and partners so everyone can work together on farm operations.

Learn more on About

Support services

Reliable help

FarmLife offers support services designed to keep your team moving. Whether you need fast answers, trusted advice, or updates on system improvements, support is always within reach.

  • Help Center: Find how-to articles, troubleshooting guides, and best practices for FarmLife tools.
  • Contact Support: Raise a ticket or message the FarmLife support team when your farm needs immediate attention.
  • Product Updates: Stay informed about new features, security upgrades, and platform improvements.
